How these numbers are calculated

Quotes. One quote per quote number (WD-, or WS- for Sintra). A part such as /1 or -1 is a quote of its own; RC (refabrication charged to the client) counts as a sale; RE is left out; -10 and similar mark factory days and belong to the main quote. Both CRM boards are read, the old one until 14 Aug and the new one after; a quote on both counts once.

Dates. Sent: the day the quote entered Orçamento enviado, or was created at that stage. Measured: it reached Orçamento retificado. Won and lost: the day the stage changed to Adjudicado or Não adjudicado. Lisbon time, from November 2025, when the CRM's change history starts. The current month is always incomplete.

Values. Valor inicial for quotes sent, Valor retificado for measured ones; won and lost use Valor retificado, or Valor inicial when there was none. Quotes without a value count but add no €. A few values that are clearly wrong (a missing decimal point, or a value the data-quality register lists as wrong) are left out of €. Totals are the sum of the rows shown.

Salesperson is the Orçamentista. Grey names are not in the current B2C team; their quotes are counted all the same.

Settled months are the run of months from November 2025 in which no more than 5% of the quotes sent are still open. After that run, a month's rates can still change, by at most the share still open.

Not in the CRM. Quotes sent by email enter the CRM only once won, so "Quotes sent" is understated and "Won overall" reads higher than reality. "Won after measurement" is barely affected. B2B is not included.

What these numbers are not. The dates are when someone updated the CRM, not when the quote went out or the sale closed: a salesperson catching up on several quotes at once makes them all look sent that day. These are quote values, not the sales record: bonuses and commissions come from the sales board. And the history starts in November 2025, so there is no year-on-year comparison before November 2026.

Checked against the sales board on 24 Oct 2026, sale by sale: Filipa, July: 22 of 22 match. Joana, June: 39 of 44; the other 5 have a different adjudication date or salesperson on the sales board.

1–3 July 2026: about 290 older quotes were closed as lost in one clean-up, so July's lost figure is high.

How these numbers are counted
  • One lead = one item created on B2C "Novo". Copies made when a quote is adjudicated are not counted.
  • The channel comes from the Canal column, set by the agency when the lead arrives.
  • Manual = added by someone on the team: phone, email, showroom, or a new quote for an existing client.
  • Counting starts 29.09, the first full day of Canal. Days run midnight to midnight, Lisbon time; today is added tomorrow.

Data notes
Points marked "don't rely on". Two kinds. On the two follow-up metrics, a clean-up on the old board refreshed the contact dates on most records — around 08 Jul, and again 4–16 Aug — so those weeks look better than the work behind them. On "loss reason" and "value filled", the 02 Jun point is a reconstructed baseline rather than a daily reading, and the 57% value-filled figure has no source we can check.

Where the lines break. On 17 Aug 2026 these metrics moved to the new board and started counting a real client contact instead of any edit to the record. "Sent quotes touched" changed again on 26 Aug: quotes with no contact date are now left out instead of counted as untouched, because most new leads arrive without one. Both sides of every break are kept.

Counting. Loss reasons span both boards over 2445 lost deals, with the old board's duplicates removed. Blank or "Escolha" counts as unfilled; "N/A" counts as unassigned. The 02 Jun "stalled" figure covers a much wider population than today, so it is a baseline only and stays off the line.
