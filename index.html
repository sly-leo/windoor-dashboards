How these numbers are calculated

Quotes. One quote per quote number (WD-, or WS- for Sintra). A part such as /1 or -1 is a quote of its own; RC (refabrication charged to the client) counts as a sale; RE is left out; -10 and similar mark factory days and belong to the main quote. Both CRM boards are read, the old one until 14 Aug and the new one after; a quote on both counts once.

Dates. Sent: the day the quote entered Orçamento enviado, or was created at that stage. Measured: it reached Orçamento retificado or Medidas OK, or its measurement record is Finalizado (the record has no history, so the Rectified line in Activity still counts Orçamento retificado only). Won and lost: the day the stage changed to Adjudicado or Não adjudicado. A quote counts as won only while it is still at Adjudicado in the CRM: one moved back is counted as lost or open again. Lisbon time, from November 2025, when the CRM's change history starts. The current month is always incomplete.

Values. Valor inicial for quotes sent, Valor retificado for measured ones (Valor inicial when there is none); won and lost use Valor retificado, or Valor inicial when there was none. Quotes without a value count but add no €. A few values that are clearly wrong (a missing decimal point, or a value the data-quality register lists as wrong) are left out of €. Totals are the sum of the rows shown.

Salesperson is the Orçamentista. People not in the current B2C team are folded under each table (open them to see their rows in grey); their quotes are counted all the same, in Team too.

Settled months are the run of months from November 2025 in which no more than 5% of the quotes sent are still open. After that run, a month's rates can still change, by at most the share still open.

Not in the CRM. Quotes sent by email enter the CRM only once won, so "Quotes sent" is understated and "Won overall" reads higher than reality. "Won after measurement" is barely affected. B2B is not included.

What these numbers are not. The dates are when someone updated the CRM, not when the quote went out or the sale closed: a salesperson catching up on several quotes at once makes them all look sent that day. These are quote values, not the sales record: bonuses and commissions come from the sales board. And the history starts in November 2025, so there is no year-on-year comparison before November 2026.

Checked against the sales board on 24 Oct 2026, sale by sale: Filipa, July: 22 of 22 match. Joana, June: 39 of 44; the other 5 have a different adjudication date or salesperson on the sales board.

1–3 July 2026: about 290 older quotes were closed as lost in one clean-up, so July's lost figure is high.

Assumptions behind the numbers
  • A recovered quote is measured, then wins at today's rate for measured quotes in its tier (A 72%, B 75%, C 75%). The data does not prove this: quotes lost before measurement may be weaker leads.
  • A recovered win is worth today's average measured win in its tier (A €7,910, B €2,945, C €1,023).
  • Volumes are the last 12 months, so results are per year. You cannot recover more quotes than were lost before measurement (A 92, B 389, C 453).
  • Each recovered quote needs one measurement visit. Visits are shown so capacity can be checked; their cost is not included.
  • Blended conversion is wins ÷ all quotes sent in the 12 months, every tier including quotes with no value. Open quotes count as not won, so it reads lower than for months that have closed.
  • Revenue, not margin.
How these numbers are calculated

Quotes. The 3,049 B2C quotes sent Nov 2025 – Oct 2026, counted exactly as on the Quotes tab: measured means the measurement record is finished, or the quote reached Medidas OK or Orçamento retificado; won means it is at Adjudicado in the CRM today. The window moves forward each month.

Tiers. A from €4,500, B €1,500 to €4,499, C below €1,500. A quote's tier is its initial value as recorded before it was won or lost; when none was entered, the value it closed at. 623 quotes have no value at all: they count in the blended conversion and in no tier.

€ won is the rectified value of won quotes, or the initial value when there is none. These are CRM quote values, not the sales record, and revenue, not margin.

Data notes
Points marked "don't rely on". Two kinds. On the two follow-up metrics, a clean-up on the old board refreshed the contact dates on most records — around 08 Jul, and again 4–16 Aug — so those weeks look better than the work behind them. On "loss reason" and "value filled", the 02 Jun point is a reconstructed baseline rather than a daily reading, and the 57% value-filled figure has no source we can check.

Where the lines break. On 17 Aug 2026 these metrics moved to the new board and started counting a real client contact instead of any edit to the record. "Sent quotes touched" changed again on 26 Aug: quotes with no contact date are now left out instead of counted as untouched, because most new leads arrive without one. Both sides of every break are kept.

Counting. Loss reasons span both boards over 2578 lost deals, with the old board's duplicates removed. Blank or "Escolha" counts as unfilled; "N/A" counts as unassigned. The 02 Jun "stalled" figure covers a much wider population than today, so it is a baseline only and stays off the line.
