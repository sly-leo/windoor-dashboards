How these numbers are counted
  • One lead = one item created on B2C "Novo". Copies made when a quote is adjudicated are not counted.
  • The channel comes from the Canal column, set by the agency when the lead arrives.
  • Manual = added by someone on the team: phone, email, showroom, or a new quote for an existing client.
  • Counting starts 29.09, the first full day of Canal. Days run midnight to midnight, Lisbon time; today is added tomorrow.

Data notes
Points marked "don't rely on". Two kinds. On the two follow-up metrics, a clean-up on the old board refreshed the contact dates on most records — around 08 Jul, and again 4–16 Aug — so those weeks look better than the work behind them. On "loss reason" and "value filled", the 02 Jun point is a reconstructed baseline rather than a daily reading, and the 57% value-filled figure has no source we can check.

Where the lines break. On 17 Aug 2026 these metrics moved to the new board and started counting a real client contact instead of any edit to the record. "Sent quotes touched" changed again on 26 Aug: quotes with no contact date are now left out instead of counted as untouched, because most new leads arrive without one. Both sides of every break are kept.

Counting. Loss reasons span both boards over 2420 lost deals, with the old board's duplicates removed. Blank or "Escolha" counts as unfilled; "N/A" counts as unassigned. The 02 Jun "stalled" figure covers a much wider population than today, so it is a baseline only and stays off the line.
